TITLE SLAM + LOWER THIRD

Fusion macros · type your word, pick the accent

  1. Title Slam: the shard fan and wedge hit in a frame, the word smears in at 1.7×, overshoots and settles. It drains off to reveal the shot.
  2. Lower Third: the plate slams from the left edge with an overshoot, then the name and the role chip. Out: it drains left.
  3. Fields: WORD · NAME · ROLE · NUMBER · ACCENT. Spacing and tilt are locked.

Tracking (.14em), tilt (−4°) and the split size are built in, so you can't set them wrong.

CHROMA BURST

adjustment clip · 10 frames

  1. Drop it on V2 starting on the cut.
  2. Red and cyan rip apart about 26px with two slice glitches, then collapse back in 10 frames.
  3. Use it on input moments only: a cut, a hit, a drop. Never on idle.

The work itself stays clean. Once the burst is over, nothing is left on the image.

what this preview is

These are drawn live in the browser from the site's rules, so the frames you're seeing are what the real files will look like. Once the look is right, the same drawing code renders the 4K ProRes clips and the PNGs. The Fusion versions are built to match frame for frame.

  • Notes like "hits too soft", "hold longer" or "the chain's too thin" go straight into these numbers.
  • Frames are a pure function of the clock, so every frame you scrub is the exact frame that will render.
