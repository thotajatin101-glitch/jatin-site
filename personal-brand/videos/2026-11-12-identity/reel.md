# Taste is what you cut — Insta reel, round 3 (2026-11-21)

His note: "a quicker shorter version with a more impactful script that grabs audience's attention and keeps it,
forget about youtube for now let's work on insta only." The YouTube cut (`script.md`) is parked.

**Length: ~25s, ~70 spoken words.** One idea only: *taste is what you cut.* Everything else from round 2 is gone.

How it holds people:
1. **Frame 1 is the hook.** No intro, no "hey guys". The line and the on-screen text hit together.
2. **An open loop at 0:05** ("one thing turned it into this") so they wait for the answer.
3. **A cut or a hit every 1–2s.** Nothing on screen holds longer than 2s until the last line.
4. **The answer is the twist**, not "consume more": it's cutting.
5. **It loops.** The last frame cuts to the black slate the first frame starts on, so a replay feels like one video.

Captions burned in for every line: most people watch muted.
