Preview: https://claude.ai/artifact/PLPAHemou7FfEHtKmmejhG (private). Source: `index.html` here (Cyberway embedded,

## Round 1 — 2026-11-09

**His notes, in his words:** "this website created my own design identity without realizing and i mean to keep it
and use in every post and video edit theme" → then "how is the asset kit gonna look like ?" → "i want to visually see
how it works first".

**Built:** a live preview, before any real files. Every piece runs in the browser on the 24fps clock as a pure
function of the frame, so scrubbing shows the exact frame a render would give:
- **Slam transition** (14f overlay): trace 0–4, white 5, slam 6 (1.14 scale, wide split), cut under 7, drain 8–13.
  Seven plates: accent field, black, support colour, light, black, accent field, support colour.
- **Chain snap** (40f): float 0–13, yank 14, thud, strain with slices, white frame 22, debris on 2s. The grade
  carries the metaphor: B/W while chained, full colour from the break.
- **Title slam + lower third** (Fusion macros): shard fan + wedge slam, word smears in at 1.7×, drains off to the
  shot; the plate slams in from the left with overshoot.
- **Chroma burst** (10f on the cut): 26px red/cyan rip collapsing, two slice glitches on 2s.
- **Sticker sheet** on alpha checkerboard: ring, bar, strand, debris, P5 star, sparkle, bolt, wedge, copy plate,
  split type, VT323 prompt.
- One **accent** switch recolours everything (the one control every macro will have).

**Changed while checking frames:** the slam first read as one cyan sheet with one black stripe (three of five
plates were the accent), so it went to seven plates in clashing colours with heavier ink rules. The chain snap ran
first on the silver-jacket shot, which is nearly monochrome already, so the B/W → colour break didn't read; it now
runs on the warm dance edit.
